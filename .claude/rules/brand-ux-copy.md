# Brand, UX, and Copy Rules

Kova should communicate like a premium SaaS built for real business owners, not like a generic POS template.

## Brand positioning

Kova is:

- A POS system.
- An inventory control layer.
- A business analytics tool.
- A simple way for SMB owners to understand sales, products, money, and operations.

Kova is not:

- A generic cash register.
- A demo dashboard.
- A cheap-looking admin panel.
- A vague "AI-powered" product without clear value.

## Copy principles

Use Spanish/es-MX by default for product and landing copy.

Good Kova copy should be clear, specific, confident, human, business-owner friendly, and focused on outcomes: control, clarity, speed, better decisions, and fewer mistakes.

Avoid clichés, overpromising, corporate filler, too much technical wording, and claims unsupported by the product.

## Pricing copy

Do not change pricing amount, currency, billing interval, or plan name without verifying Stripe/config/code/planning docs first.
