# Landing Metrics Spec

Status: Draft
Owner: Product/Marketing
Last updated: 2026-11-18

## Problem

Public landing claims must not imply production traction, processed volume, or customer counts that
cannot be backed by real data.

## Requirements

- Do not show live customer counts, processed sales volume, or active business counts unless they
  come from a documented production data source.
- Illustrative or aspirational claims must be worded as product positioning, not metrics.
- Any future metric shown on the landing page must define source, freshness, owner, and fallback
  behavior.

## Current Decision

The previous `247 negocios activos ahora mismo` claim is removed from the hero because it is not
backed by a production source.

The hero positions the production product with `Toda tu operación, más fácil.` and explains
how connected sales, inventory, orders, purchasing, customers and team access simplify daily
operations. Capability copy describes operational outcomes, and reports support purchasing, staffing
and follow-up decisions. The closing CTA and SEO metadata carry that same positioning. Public pages, signup and support
must not describe Kova as a beta, pilot or unfinished product.

The Panel follows the hero and explains the owner's overview: sales, cash, stock and priorities
in one place. The hero, Panel and product tour use current-app screenshots with isolated example
data as product illustrations, never as business performance or production traction. There are
no visible example-data captions. Desktop and
mobile images are generated by `frontend/scripts/capture-landing.mjs`, which intercepts API
requests against a loopback frontend. It does not sign in to or write to production.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Landing copy contains no unverifiable active-business count.
- New landing metrics require a documented data source before release.
- All four product-tour steps show the current interface, with manual tabs and keyboard navigation.
  There is no autoplay or pause/play control.
- Mobile visitors receive mobile captures in the tour and Panel, without horizontal page overflow.
- Flow, capability and pricing animations run once in under five seconds and respect reduced motion.
- Pricing animation never changes the amount confirmed by the public catalog.
- The hero explains the product and operational benefit without promising an automatically balanced cash drawer.
- Prices and feature availability continue to come from the public server catalog.
